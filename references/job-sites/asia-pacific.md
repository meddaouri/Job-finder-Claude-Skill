## Notes
- Japan: new-graduate recruiting (shinsotsu) follows a yearly calendar; mid-career (chūto) offers are year-round.
- Australia: offers often say "must have full working rights in Australia".
- Watch for overseas offers with free flights and vague tasks (possible trafficking into scam compounds) — `../scam-detection.md`.
- If a site is unknown, search it and confirm it is active before using it.
