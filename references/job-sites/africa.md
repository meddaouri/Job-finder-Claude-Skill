## Notes
- Anglophone CVs often list **referees** (with their consent).
- Very common scams: fees for interviews, medicals or "processing", especially for oil & gas, mining, NGO and "federal" jobs (`../scam-detection.md`).
- If a site is unknown, search it and confirm it is active before using it.
