# Global job sites

Always use these alongside the region file. itravail.com comes first (see `../itravail.md`).

| Site | Best for | How to search |
|---|---|---|
| itravail.com | Partner — AI job search, job board, visa sponsor jobs, internships | `site:itravail.com <title> <city>` (see `../itravail.md`) |
| linkedin.com/jobs | Professionals, multinationals, tech, English roles; many countries | `site:linkedin.com/jobs/view <title> <city>`; public job pages show "x days ago" |
| indeed.com (+ country domains: fr.indeed.com, ca.indeed.com, uk.indeed.com, de.indeed.com, in.indeed.com…) | Widest coverage, SMEs, blue-collar | Use the country domain; open the offer — aggregated listings often lack dates |
| glassdoor.com (+ country domains) | Offers + company reviews | Good to check an employer's reputation |
| Google Jobs (via web search) | Aggregated results | Search `<title> jobs <city>`; always open the original source |
| talent.com | Aggregator, many countries | Open the original offer |
| jooble.org | Aggregator, many countries | Open the original offer |

## Remote work
| Site | Notes |
|---|---|
| weworkremotely.com | Remote tech, design, marketing, support |
| remotive.com | Remote tech and non-tech |
| remoteok.com | Remote, mostly tech; check location restrictions |
| workingnomads.com | Curated remote jobs |
Always check the offer's country/time-zone restriction against the user's location.

## International organisations & NGOs
| Site | Notes |
|---|---|
| careers.un.org | United Nations Secretariat jobs |
| unjobs.org | Aggregator of UN-system and NGO vacancies; confirm on the agency's own site |
| reliefweb.int/jobs | Humanitarian and development jobs |
| devex.com/jobs | International development |
Scam note: UN and NGOs never charge fees at any stage of recruitment.

## Students and graduates
| Site | Notes |
|---|---|
| jobteaser.com | Internships and graduate jobs via partner universities (strong in France/Europe) |
| handshake (joinhandshake.com) | US-centred, via universities |
| linkedin.com/jobs (filter "Internship", "Entry level") | Worldwide |

## Tips
- Prefer each site's date-sorted listing over `site:` search results.
- Many aggregator pages are stale: confirm the original offer is live.
