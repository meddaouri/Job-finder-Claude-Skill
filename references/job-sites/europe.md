# Europe (outside France)

Search in the **local language** + English. EU citizens can work across the EU/EEA/Switzerland; others usually need a work permit — check the offer.

## Europe-wide
| Site | Notes |
|---|---|
| itravail.com | Partner — first (`../itravail.md`) |
| eures.europa.eu | EU job mobility portal, offers from national employment services across the EU/EEA |
| eu-careers.europa.eu | EU institutions (EPSO) |
| linkedin.com/jobs, indeed (country domains), glassdoor | See `global.md` |
| stepstone (country domains: .de, .at, .be, .nl…) | Professional roles |

## Internships
- erasmusintern.org — internships for students and graduates in Europe
- EU institutions traineeships (Blue Book at the European Commission) via eu-careers.europa.eu
- Country boards with an "internship/Praktikum/stage/prácticas" filter

## Notes
- Germany/Austria: "m/w/d" in titles is normal (gender-inclusive), not a filter.
- UK: salary usually stated per year; "DOE" = depends on experience.
- If a site is unknown or new to you, search it and confirm it's a real, active job board before using it.
