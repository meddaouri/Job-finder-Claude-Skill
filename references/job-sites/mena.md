## Internships
- Bayt and LinkedIn "internship" filters; large employers' graduate programmes (banks, airlines, telecoms, energy companies). Dubai: free-zone companies and multinationals advertise "Intern" roles on LinkedIn/Bayt. Check whether the internship needs a student/intern visa or university letter.

## Notes
- Gulf offers often state "visa provided", "family status", "nationality preference" — show as written; don't add.
- Salary in AED/SAR/QAR monthly, sometimes "tax-free" — show as written.
- Scam check carefully on classifieds and WhatsApp-only offers (`../scam-detection.md`).
