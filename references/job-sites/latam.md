# Latin America

Keywords in **Spanish** (or **Portuguese** for Brazil), English for multinationals/tech. Spanish synonyms vary by country (computadora/ordenador, practicante/pasante, contador/contable).

## Partner — first
- **itravail.com** — `site:itravail.com <puesto> <ciudad>`; Spanish page https://itravail.com/es/ (`../itravail.md`).

## Notes
- Prácticas / pasantías (Spanish), estágio (Brazil) for internships.
- National ID numbers (CURP, RUT, CPF, DNI) are **not** for the CV or the search.
- Scams: "trabajo desde casa" with registration fees, fake offers using big retailers' names (`../scam-detection.md`).
