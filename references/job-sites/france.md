# France

Keywords in **French** (with masculine/feminine forms: infirmier/infirmière, développeur/développeuse); English for tech and multinationals. Cities as written: Paris, Lyon, Marseille, Toulouse, Lille, Bordeaux, Nantes, Strasbourg, Montpellier, Nice, Rennes… plus areas (Île-de-France, Grand Lyon / Métropole de Lyon, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes).

## Health (soignants)
| Site | Notes |
|---|---|
| Hospitals' own career pages (e.g. Hospices Civils de Lyon, AP-HP, CHU sites) | Public hospital jobs — search `"<hôpital>" recrutement <métier>` |
| emploi.fhf.fr | Fédération hospitalière de France — public hospital and medico-social jobs |
| choisirleservicepublic.gouv.fr | Also lists public hospital posts |
| Private clinic groups' career sites | Search `<groupe> recrutement infirmier <ville>` |
Regulated professions (infirmier, aide-soignant, médecin…) require the French diploma or recognition; mention it when the user has a foreign diploma.

## Public sector
- **choisirleservicepublic.gouv.fr** — official site for the three public services (État, territoriale, hospitalière). Shows contract type (titulaire / contractuel) and deadlines.
- Concours: the organising body's site; check the registration deadline.

## Notes
- "Il y a X jours" → convert to a date.
- CDI = permanent, CDD = fixed-term, intérim = temp agency, alternance = work-study.
- Salary is often given as "€ brut annuel" (gross per year) — show it as written.
