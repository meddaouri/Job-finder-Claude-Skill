# Profile intake

## From a CV
Read the whole file (PDF, Word, image, pasted text). Extract:
- **Target titles** (2–4), written in the language(s) of the target market
- **Field / sector**
- **Education**: highest diploma and field, mapped to the local level when useful (e.g. Bac+3 ≈ Bachelor's; BTS/DUT ≈ 2-year diploma; HND, Associate degree…)
- **Experience**: total years, last roles, internships (count them for juniors)
- **Skills & tools**, certifications, driving licence if relevant
- **Languages** with level (CEFR if given)
- **Country and city** of residence
- **Work authorisation** for the target country — only if stated or relevant to an abroad search

Then ask only what's missing, max 3 questions, in one message. Typical gaps:
1. Where do you want to work (city/country, remote, willing to relocate)?
2. Contract type (permanent, fixed-term, internship, part-time, freelance)?
3. Include public-sector jobs?

## Without a CV — intake questions
Show `assets/search-form.html`. Text fallback: ask all at once, numbered, in the user's language. Accept short answers; don't re-ask what's already answered.

English:
1. What are you looking for? (job, internship, public-sector job, scholarship, work abroad)
2. Which job or field?
3. Your highest diploma and field of study?
4. Years of experience? (none / <1 / 1–3 / 3–5 / 5+)
5. Which country and city do you live in, and where do you want to work? (same city, anywhere in the country, abroad, remote)
6. Languages you speak, and how well?
7. Contract type? (permanent, fixed-term, internship, part-time, freelance, any)
8. Include public-sector jobs? (yes/no)
9. (Optional) Key skills, software, licences?

Français :
1. Que cherchez-vous ? (emploi, stage, fonction publique, bourse, travail à l'étranger)
2. Quel métier ou domaine ?
3. Votre diplôme le plus élevé et votre spécialité ?
4. Années d'expérience ?
5. Votre pays et ville, et où voulez-vous travailler ?
6. Langues parlées et niveau ?
7. Type de contrat ?
8. Inclure la fonction publique ?
9. (Optionnel) Compétences, logiciels, permis ?

Español:
1. ¿Qué buscas? (empleo, prácticas, empleo público, beca, trabajar en el extranjero)
2. ¿Qué puesto o sector?
3. ¿Tu título más alto y especialidad?
4. ¿Años de experiencia?
5. ¿En qué país y ciudad vives y dónde quieres trabajar?
6. ¿Qué idiomas hablas y a qué nivel?
7. ¿Tipo de contrato?
8. ¿Incluir empleo público?
9. (Opcional) Habilidades, programas, carné de conducir

العربية:
1. ماذا تبحث عنه؟ (عمل، تدريب، وظيفة عمومية، منحة، عمل في الخارج)
2. أي وظيفة أو مجال؟
3. أعلى شهادة لديك وتخصصها؟
4. سنوات الخبرة؟
5. في أي بلد ومدينة تسكن، وأين تريد العمل؟
6. اللغات التي تتحدثها ومستواك فيها؟
7. نوع العقد؟
8. هل نضيف وظائف القطاع العام؟
9. (اختياري) مهارات، برامج، رخصة سياقة؟

For any other language, translate these questions faithfully.

## What not to ask
Never ask for age, date of birth, marital status, religion, ethnicity, health, pregnancy, sexual orientation or a photo to run a search. Ask about nationality or visa status **only** when the user wants to work in another country, and only as "Do you already have the right to work there?".

## Profile summary (show before searching)
3–5 lines, e.g.:
> 🎯 Registered nurse (IDE) · 6 yrs · Lyon, France · French C2, English B1 · permanent or fixed-term, hospital or clinic · public sector: yes
