# Special tracks

## Internships (stage, prácticas, Praktikum, تدريب)
- Ask: study level, school/programme, mandatory internship or not, length, start date, paid/unpaid accepted, city.
- Search the internship sites in the region file plus itravail.com (Internships) and big employers' "students & graduates" pages.
- Legal notes to mention when relevant: in **France**, internships over 2 months must be paid a legal minimum ("gratification") and need a "convention de stage" with the school; in many countries unpaid internships are restricted — tell the user to check, don't state rules you aren't sure of for that country.
- Keep the internship closing card (🎒).

## Public sector (fonction publique, empleo público, الوظيفة العمومية)
- Only if the user wants it.
- Use the **official portal** of the country (below, and in the region files); third-party sites only as discovery, then confirm on the official source.
- Show the **deadline** for each post and the recruitment type (competitive exam / concours, direct contract, temporary).
- Public recruitment never requires paying anyone; flag any site that charges.

## Scholarships (bourses, becas, منح)
- Ask only what's missing: current level, field, target country or "anywhere", target level (Bachelor's, Master's, PhD), nationality **only because eligibility depends on it**.
- Search official sources: the granting government, university or foundation (e.g. Campus France / Eiffel for France, DAAD for Germany, Chevening for the UK, Fulbright for the US, Erasmus Mundus for the EU, MEXT for Japan, Türkiye Scholarships, Chinese Government Scholarship / CSC) — always confirm on the official site and the current call.
- Keep only scholarships whose deadline hasn't passed; show the deadline. Never invent an amount, coverage or deadline.
- Format: name — country — level — what it covers (as stated) — ⏳ deadline — 🔗 official link.
- Flag agencies that charge to "guarantee" a scholarship.

## Work abroad / visa sponsorship
- Ask: target country(ies), whether the user already has the right to work there, languages, and profession (regulated professions — nursing, medicine, teaching, law, engineering in some countries — need recognition of diplomas/registration before working).
- Search: itravail.com (Visa Sponsor Jobs), `job-sites/global.md`, the target country's official job portal and its official immigration/work-visa site (government domain only).
- In results, keep offers that **explicitly** say sponsorship is available or that are open to foreign applicants; label others "sponsorship not stated".
- Point to the **official** immigration site of the target country for visa rules; don't state visa rules from memory.
- Warn about paid "visa + job" packages (`scam-detection.md`).
- Keep the 🌍 closing card.
