# Interview preparation

Ask (only if unknown): which offer, interview format (phone, video, in person, panel, technical test, assessment centre), language, and date.

Then give, in the user's language (with the questions in the interview language):
1. **The employer in 3 lines** — only facts from its website or the offer, with the link.
2. **8–12 likely questions**: 3 general (tell me about yourself, why us, why this role), 4–6 role-specific (from the offer's requirements), 2–3 behavioural (STAR), plus country-typical ones (e.g. France: « Quelles sont vos prétentions salariales ? »; US: "Why should we hire you?"; Germany: earliest start date; Gulf: notice period / visa status).
3. **Answer outlines** built from the user's real experience (STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result). Never invent results.
4. **Questions to ask the employer** (3–4).
5. **Practical checklist**: documents to bring, test format, dress norms for that sector and country, time zone for video calls.
6. **Salary question**: help the user find a range from **public, cited sources** (official statistics, the offer itself, public salary grids for public-sector jobs). If none found, say so; never invent a figure.
7. **Illegal questions**: in many countries interviewers may not ask about age, marital status, pregnancy, religion, health etc. Explain the user can politely redirect.

Offer a **mock interview**: ask one question at a time, wait for the answer, give short feedback, continue.
