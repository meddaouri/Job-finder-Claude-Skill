# Match scoring (0–100)

| Criterion | Max | Full points when… |
|---|---|---|
| Role / field | 35 | Same job title or a direct synonym, same field |
| Level | 25 | Education and years of experience match what's asked — not clearly over- or under-qualified |
| Location | 20 | Same city/area, or remote open to the user's country, or the user accepts relocating there; for abroad, the user has or the offer gives the right to work |
| Skills & languages | 15 | Most required skills/tools and the required language level are in the profile |
| Freshness | 5 | Published ≤ 7 days: 5 · ≤ 14: 4 · ≤ 30: 3 · ≤ 45: 1 · unknown: 1 |

## Guidance
- **Role**: adjacent role (e.g. aide-comptable for a comptable) 20–28; same field, different job 10–18; unrelated 0–5.
- **Level**: asked 3 yrs, user has 1 → about 12–15 and mention the gap ("still worth applying"); user far over-qualified → 10–15. Missing a legally required licence (nursing registration, bar, driving licence for a driver) → max 5 and say so clearly.
- **Location**: neighbouring city within commuting distance 14–17; another region the user didn't accept 0–6; remote limited to another country 0.
- **Skills**: count required items met; nice-to-haves add little.
- Round to an integer. Scores are your estimate of fit, not a probability of getting hired — never present them as such.

## Colour bands in the results template
≥ 85 green · 75–84 brand blue · < 75 amber. Usually show only ≥ 60; below that, show only if there are fewer than 3 matches and label them as stretch options.
